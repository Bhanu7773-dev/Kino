## Features

### Streaming
- 🎬 **Direct torrent streaming**: play instantly without waiting for downloads.
- 📶 **Wi-Fi and hotspot**: high-speed local P2P streaming with zero bufferbloat.
- ⬇️ **Offline downloads**: save movies locally and watch without a connection.

### Player
- 🗂️ **Rich player**: gesture scrubbing, brightness and volume control, and Picture-in-Picture mode.

### LapCast `BETA`
- 💻 **Cast to your laptop browser**: no PC app needed. Your phone serves the catalog and video, and your laptop just opens a page.
- 🎯 **Spatial Gyro Wand**: aim your phone like a laser pointer to control the cursor.
- 🖱️ **Touchpad and D-Pad**: seamless cursor control with edge auto-scrolling.
- ⚡ **0 ms remote deck**: instant play, pause, seek and volume response.

### Privacy
- 📴 **No accounts, no cloud logins, no ads.**
- 🆓 **Free and open source.**

## Getting started

### Download
Grab the latest APK from the [Releases](../../releases) page and install it on your Android device.

### Build from source
```bash
git clone https://github.com/<your-username>/<your-repo>.git
cd <your-repo>
flutter pub get
flutter run
```
Requires the [Flutter SDK](https://docs.flutter.dev/get-started/install) and an Android device or emulator.

## Using LapCast

1. Connect your phone and laptop to the same Wi-Fi network, or turn on the phone's hotspot and join it from the laptop.
2. Open LapCast in StreamX on your phone.
3. Open the address shown on your phone in your laptop's browser.
4. Pick something to watch, then use your phone as the remote with the gyro wand, touchpad or D-Pad.
